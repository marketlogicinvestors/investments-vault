What actually happened · as of 31 Dec 2024

Closed 3 May 2024. Sunoco FY2024: Adjusted EBITDA $1,563m excluding transaction costs, DCF as adjusted $1,081m, 136.2m units, leverage 4.1x at 31 Dec 2024. FY2024 DCF per unit of $7.94 on the year-end unit count compares with $7.87 standalone in 2023, with only eight months of NuStar and before most synergies. Purchase accounting: total consideration $2,850m, debt assumed $3,500m at fair value, preferred $801m, goodwill $16m.

Sunoco LP / Parkland Corporation (2025)

Announced 2025-05-05 · closed 2025-10-31 · workbook tab M02 SUN-Parkland 2025

Not an LBO. Cross-border cash-and-units acquisition by plan of arrangement: C$19.80 cash plus 0.295 SunocoCorp units per Parkland share, funded with $1.9bn of senior notes and $1.5bn of 7.875% perpetual preferred. Not an LBO. The model tests the two numbers Sunoco put on the table: 10%+ DCF-per-unit accretion by year 3 and a return to 4.0x leverage. Parkland reports in C$ under IFRS; EBITDA and net debt are taken on Parkland's own lease-excluded "Leverage" basis so they are comparable with Sunoco's US GAAP figures.

These two transactions were strategic acquisitions by a listed master limited partnership, so the LBO template used elsewhere in this book does not apply. The models here are the ones a Sunoco board would have seen: does the deal raise distributable cash flow per unit after the units issued and the financing cost, and where does leverage land against the 4.0x target. Accretion is measured against a flat standalone base; move the standalone growth slider to change that. Every input carries a tag; assumptions (A) are the sliders to distrust first. The workbook linked in the header carries the same two models as tabs M01 and M02. Not investment advice.
