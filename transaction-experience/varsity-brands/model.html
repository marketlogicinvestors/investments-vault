Underwritten case = the template run on the entry structure and stated assumptions over a five-year hold; interest is charged on opening debt and all levered free cash flow accumulates as cash, so returns are slightly flattered versus a model with a debt sweep. Actual = the realised exit where one is disclosed. Every input above carries a tag; assumptions (A) are the sliders to distrust first. The workbook linked in the header carries the same eleven paper models plus four full three-statement LBO builds. Not investment advice.
