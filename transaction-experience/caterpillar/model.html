Actual outcome · no exit

Never exited. Bucyrus contributed $2,524m of sales and a $(96)m operating loss in the 2011 stub, $4,758m and $257m in 2012. Caterpillar Resource Industries segment then fell from $21.2bn sales / $4.3bn profit (2012) to $5.7bn / $(1,047)m (2016), the year of the $595m Surface Mining & Technology goodwill impairment and $132m intangible write-down.

Note. Not a sponsor deal. The notes were Caterpillar corporate debt at ~2.9%, and the 'equity' was balance-sheet cash. The template is used to show what the price required: EBITDA roughly doubling on a 13.8x entry. The cycle turned the other way within eighteen months.

Underwritten case = the template run on the entry structure and stated assumptions over a five-year hold; interest is charged on opening debt and all levered free cash flow accumulates as cash, so returns are slightly flattered versus a model with a debt sweep. Actual = the realised exit where one is disclosed. Every input above carries a tag; assumptions (A) are the sliders to distrust first.
