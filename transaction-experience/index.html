Transaction Experience

Twelve client relationships across twenty years of enterprise transformation and M&A integration work, covering 76 transactions. Each file reconstructs the client's deal history in full from the primary record — SEC filings, court opinions, takeover-code documents, trademark files and government reports — with consideration, financing, purchase accounting, advisers, principals and outcomes, and a linked source for every figure. Where a file reaches back before the engagement, it is to give the transactions worked their full context.

This is a record of client transactions worked, not a portfolio of investments underwritten. Nothing here carries a score or a recommendation; the deal books under VC, SPAC and PE do that. What these files carry instead is what the filings actually said, including where the commonly repeated version of a deal is wrong.

01
Automotive · 1998 – 2009 · ML-TXN-DCX

DaimlerChrysler

Daimler-Benz AG · Chrysler Corporation · DaimlerChrysler AG · Cerberus Capital Management

Daimler-Benz and Chrysler combined in 1998 in a three-step exchange offer and Delaware merger into a new German stock corporation, accounted for as a pooling of interests so no goodwill was ever recorded. The "merger of equals" representation produced a $300m securities class action settlement and the Tracinda trial. Chrysler was carved out to Cerberus in 2007 at a net cash outflow to Daimler, and the retained 19.9% was relinquished for no consideration in 2009.

General Motors

General Motors Corporation · Motors Liquidation Company · General Motors Company · NYSE: GM

Chapter 11 on 1 June 2009, a Section 363 free-and-clear sale approved 5 July and closed 10 July, and a capital structure rebuilt around Treasury, Canada, the UAW VEBA and the unsecured bondholders. The November 2010 IPO raised ~$23.1bn. Treasury exited on 9 December 2013 having recovered ~$39bn of $49.5bn.

Distribution & e-commerce · 2002 – 2016 · ML-TXN-NAV

Navarre / Speed Commerce

Navarre Corporation · NASDAQ: NAVR → SPDC · Minneapolis / Richardson, TX

Navarre bought Encore out of bankruptcy for ~$10m in 2002 and FUNimation for $117.8m in 2005 on a $165m GE Capital facility. It impaired $73.4m of goodwill in one quarter of FY2009, sold FUNimation for $24.0m in 2011, reverse-merged with SpeedFC in 2012, sold the legacy distribution business for a stated $15m that realised ~$5m, levered $100m for Fifth Gear, and was foreclosed on in June 2016.

Agriculture & chemicals · 1993 – 2018 · ML-TXN-MOS

Mosaic

The Mosaic Company · NYSE: MOS · Cargill, Incorporated · IMC Global Inc.

Cargill contributed its crop nutrition business into Global Nutrition Solutions, which simultaneously merged with IMC Global in October 2004; Cargill took 66.5% and 256,041,277 shares. The 2011 split-off unwound that stake tax-free under Section 355 by exchanging shares for Cargill stock and Cargill debt. Vale Fertilizantes followed in 2018, renegotiated downward days before closing.

How these files are organised

Every transaction is tagged against one classification. The taxonomy below is the one used throughout the files and is the fastest way in if you are looking for a structure rather than a company.

Public M&AStock-for-stock mergers of equals and cash mergers or tender offers for listed targets. DaimlerChrysler, Stanley/Black & Decker, Bucyrus, Varsity Spirit.
Private equitySponsor entries and exits: carve-outs to sponsors, sponsor-to-strategic sales, secondary buyouts and take-privates. Chrysler/Cerberus, Pacific Cycle, EMD, the Varsity chain.
Corporate carve-outDivisions and segments sold out of a listed parent. Direct Energy, Stanley Security, Weir Oil & Gas, Dorel Sports, Vale Fertilizantes.
Distressed & insolvencySection 363 sales, bankruptcy auctions and lender enforcement. General Motors, Encore, Schwinn/GT, Iron Horse, the Speed Commerce foreclosure.
Structured & tax-drivenSplit-offs, contributed combinations, minority buy-ins and Up-C vehicles. Cargill/Mosaic, Verizon/Vodafone, SunocoCorp.
MLP & partnershipRoll-ups inside a sponsored master limited partnership complex. Enable, Crestwood, NuStar, WTG, Parkland.
Cross-borderTransactions run under a non-US takeover code or a Canadian plan of arrangement. Niscayah, ERA Mining, Parkland, Caloi.
Terminated & contestedSigned deals that did not complete, and the litigation that followed. Dorel/Cerberus, Energy Transfer/Williams, Tracinda, Siwei.
