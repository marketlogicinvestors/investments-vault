Chapter 11 filed 30 Apr 2009. First-lien lenders (~$6.9bn) took $2.0bn cash (~29c) in the 363 sale to New Chrysler; second lien and equity recovered nothing. 2008 EBITDA was $0.3bn on $55.9bn revenue (Chrysler restructuring plan to Treasury, Feb-2009). Sponsor equity: total loss.

Underwritten case = the template run on the entry structure and stated assumptions over a five-year hold; interest is charged on opening debt and all levered free cash flow accumulates as cash, so returns are slightly flattered versus a model with a debt sweep. Actual = the realised exit where one is disclosed. Every input above carries a tag; assumptions (A) are the sliders to distrust first.
